# Support & contact

## For users

- **Privacy policy (public):** open from the app (**Settings → Privacy Policy**) or directly: `https://altervpn-production.up.railway.app/privacy.html`
- **GitHub Issues:** available only if you grant access to the repository (repo may be private)
- **Developers:** canonical policy text also in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) (keep in sync with `web/privacy.html`)

## For Google Play Console

Play may ask for **Contact email** and optionally **Website** or **Phone**.

| Field | Suggested value |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Email** | Add your **dedicated support email** (e.g. `support@yourdomain.com`) in Play Console — do not use a personal inbox if you can avoid it |
| **Website** | `https://github.com/BaasherHub/AlterVpn-` or your landing page if you create one |

> **Action:** Replace any placeholder with your real support address before going live. You do not need to put the email inside the app unless you want a “Contact” screen later.

## Security

To report a **security vulnerability**, do not use public issues — see **[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)**.
