AlterVPN Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

AlterVPN (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a privacy-focused VPN client. This policy describes how we handle information when you use the app.

Data We Collect

  • No account required. You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use AlterVPN.
  • Local storage only. The app stores preferences (e.g. theme, server selection, session timers) on your device using standard system storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • VPN connection. When you connect, your traffic is routed through the selected VPN server. We do not log, monitor, or store your VPN traffic.

Third-Party Services

  • AdMob (Google). If you use the rewarded ad feature to unlock free connection time, Google AdMob may collect data according to its Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.
  • VPN servers. Your traffic passes through third-party VPN infrastructure. Connect only to servers you trust.

Data Safety

AlterVPN does not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the contact information on the Google Play store listing for AlterVPN, or reach the developer through the channels listed there.
